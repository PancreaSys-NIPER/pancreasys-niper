Our Research Projects

Systems Pharmacology Approaches

Translating complex multi-omics data into clinically actionable insights for pancreatic disorders. Our research focuses on integrating genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics to develop precision medicine strategies.

Animal Models of Pancreatic Disorders

Developing and characterizing animal models to understand the pathophysiology of pancreatic exocrine disorders. These models serve as crucial tools for testing therapeutic interventions and understanding disease mechanisms.

Multi-omics Data Integration

Leveraging computational biology and bioinformatics to integrate multi-omics datasets. Our goal is to identify biomarkers and therapeutic targets for pancreatic diseases through comprehensive molecular profiling.

Translational Medicine

Bridging the gap between basic research and clinical applications. We focus on developing therapeutic strategies that can be translated from bench to bedside for improved patient outcomes.
