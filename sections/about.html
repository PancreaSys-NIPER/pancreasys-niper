About the Lab

Lab Mission

The PancreaSys Lab, based in the Department of Pharmacology and Toxicology at NIPER, S.A.S. Nagar, operates at the cutting edge of precision medicine and systems pharmacology. Under the leadership of Dr. Ujjwal Mukund Mahajan, our team integrates computational data science with wet-lab experimental biology to unravel the complexities of pancreatic exocrine disorders and oncology.

History

The PancreaSys lab was founded in April 2025 as part of the Department of Pharmacology and Toxicology at NIPER, S.A.S. Nagar. Led by PD Dr. Ujjwal Mukund Mahajan, the lab is committed to advancing precision medicine in pancreatic disorders through innovative systems pharmacology approaches.

Our Values

We are committed to doing exceptional science, creating a safe and collegial workspace, developing comprehensive scientific skills, building professional networks, and preparing our members for their next career milestone.
